Signal Fire

Most people don't skip an unfamiliar gathering because they aren't interested — they skip it because nothing tells them they're welcome. Signal Fire is the missing signal: a durable, QR-coded marker at the beach court, the garden, the park. Scan it and you see what's happening here, hosted by a real person, and you walk in already knowing you belong.
