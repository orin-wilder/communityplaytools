St. Pete Detective Club

A six-location, 2.5-mile field mystery that turned downtown St. Petersburg into the game board.

Twenty seats sold. Crews of 3 to 6 moved through physical locations while a custom mobile platform maintained shared crew state, unlocked evidence as solves accumulated, and supported an interactive endgame deduction.

Signal Fire

Place-based participation, beginning with the physical signal that says a person is welcome here.

I designed, built, and deployed Signal Fire as a Rails and Expo (React Native) product. Place-specific boards connected physical totems to hosted activity and made participation legible before someone had to cross the social threshold.

Current status: the product shipped and remains a functional live build. I chose not to keep pursuing it as an active operating business after evaluating the business model against the time and resources available. No user, revenue, or traction claim is implied.

Williams Park engagement suite

Six formats designed as one feedback system, not a shelf of disconnected demos.

The suite connects playful participation to planning input through QR sentiment tracking, an interactive idea-remix app, spatial preference mapping, gamified priority ranking, video postcards, and written vision capture.

I also designed the staffing model, phased delivery, and a downtown perception baseline that could function as a reusable “before” dataset.

Status: designed and pitched as a three-tier consulting engagement. The prospect’s budget was pulled. It was not an awarded client contract and no participation data was collected.
