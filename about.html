About Ryan Stock

Builder of systems. Connector of people. Catalyst for change.

Who I Am

For seven years, I managed high-stakes programs at Sony PlayStation, leading innovation initiatives, global summits, and payment system launches. Then I spent two years as President of Beautiful PB, a community planning nonprofit in San Diego. Most consultants pick one lane. I've run both, and that's what makes me different.

At Sony, I learned to coordinate engineering teams, navigate legal compliance, and deliver on impossible deadlines. In Pacific Beach, I figured out how to move controversial projects through city approval boards and raise $450,000 for a historic building restoration. The skills overlap more than you'd think.

Here's what I'm good at: I take problems that involve too many people with competing priorities (city officials who want one thing, developers who want another, residents who want something else) and I find a way forward. Sometimes that means building coalitions. Sometimes it means presenting data that changes the conversation. Always, it means understanding what each stakeholder actually needs versus what they say they need, and designing a solution that works for everyone.

The transition: After seven years building innovation programs at Sony and two years scaling Beautiful PB from under $1,000 to $40,000 in annual budget while raising $450,000 for a historic arts center, I'm bringing both skill sets to St. Petersburg organizations ready for transformation.

Why St. Petersburg?

I've recently relocated to St. Petersburg, drawn by the city's remarkable growth trajectory and its potential to model the future of urban innovation. The convergence of the Innovation District, downtown revitalization, and progressive mobility initiatives creates a unique opportunity to build sustainable, people-centered infrastructure.

St. Petersburg is at a critical inflection point—positioned to become a national leader in smart city development, sustainable transportation, and technology-driven civic engagement. I want to contribute to building that future.

I speak fluently across sectors—translating between "City Council," "Parking Board," and "Tech Innovation" to move high-stakes projects forward. My experience bridges Fortune 500 strategic rigor and grassroots civic leadership, giving me unique perspective on the challenges St. Petersburg faces as it scales.

My Areas of Focus in St. Pete:

  • Building cultural infrastructure for cycling and car-free living
  • Activating streets with lightweight, scalable technology
  • Facilitating community engagement for major development projects
  • Designing innovation programs that connect research, industry, and community
  • Creating data-driven approaches to mobility and urban planning
