FAQ

A few things people ask.

How quickly can you start?

After an initial conversation and agreement on scope, we can usually begin within two weeks. Faster is sometimes possible for urgent work.

Remote or on-site?

We're based in St. Petersburg and work hybrid — on-site for meetings, workshops, and stakeholder engagement, remote for planning and execution.

What's the typical engagement length?

Most run six to twelve months. Our approach is designed to transfer knowledge and build your team's capacity, so the work continues after we step back.

What do you not take on?

Not purely technical builds, not dedicated fundraising, and not short tactical work under three months. We focus on strategic initiatives that need stakeholder alignment and systems thinking.

Can you share references?

Absolutely — former Sony colleagues and community leaders from Beautiful PB and the Pacific Beach Planning Group, provided after an initial conversation.

Can you build a tool for us?

Yes. We adapt Signal Fire and our other tools to new communities, or build something new from the same playbook. Start with the form and tell us the goal.
