Frequently Asked Questions

What does an engagement typically cost?

Engagements are custom-priced based on scope, complexity, and duration. Typical 6-12 month strategic engagements range from $8,000-$15,000 per month depending on time commitment and deliverables. Let's discuss your specific needs and budget during our discovery call.

How long does it take to start?

After our initial conversation and agreement on scope, we can typically start within 2 weeks. For urgent projects, faster timelines may be possible.

Do you work remotely or on-site?

We are based in St. Petersburg and prefer a hybrid approach—combining on-site presence for critical meetings, workshops, and stakeholder engagement with remote work for planning and execution. The exact balance depends on your organization's needs and location.

What if we need you for longer than 12 months?

Engagements can be extended beyond 12 months if there's mutual value. However, my approach is designed to transfer knowledge and build internal capacity so your team can sustain the work independently. Extensions are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

What types of projects do you NOT take on?

I don't take on projects that are purely technical (requiring deep engineering expertise), purely fundraising-focused (I can support strategy but not serve as a dedicated fundraiser), or short-term tactical work under 3 months. I focus on strategic initiatives that require stakeholder alignment, systems thinking, and transformational change.

Can you provide references?

Absolutely. I'm happy to connect you with former colleagues from Sony, community leaders from Beautiful PB and the Pacific Beach Planning Group, and other stakeholders I've worked with. References are provided after our initial conversation.
