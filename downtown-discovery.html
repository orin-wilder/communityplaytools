Now building · Downtown Discovery

Always on is never an event.

Downtown already has apps, punch cards, and games that are always there, so they’re never a priority. People show up for a happening. Downtown Discovery is a local play platform built to give people a reason to be downtown tonight, on the nights our merchants need it most.

The problem

Weekends take care of themselves.

Early-week nights are quiet, and the busy nights crowd a few anchor venues. Discounts and one-off events are expensive and rarely become a habit.

That pattern comes from my early conversations with bar owners along Central Avenue. I’m collecting more merchant perspectives now, and I’d like to hear yours.

For people

A reason to go out tonight.

Something happening, with a crew, a route, and a small reward for showing up.

For businesses

A fuller room on a slow night.

Foot traffic when you need it, not another discount on a night you’re already full.

For the district

Traffic past the anchors.

Visits spread across more blocks, with data to show where people actually went.

What it is

A platform, not a single app.

Small play stations live inside neighborhood shops. Games run on top of them all the time, and timed happenings bring a crowd through on purpose.

It’s local and locally run, and it’s open to St. Pete artists and creators who want to build the next game on it. The first always-on game is a collectible sticker adventure, in development under the working name Sticker Safari.

How the happenings work is debuting on stage at 1 Million Cups. Want the preview? Let’s get coffee.
