Approach

Civic engagement should feel like play, not homework.

When taking part is a chore, the same five people decide everything. When it feels like play, the whole neighborhood shows up. That belief shapes every project we lead and every tool we build.

We take problems that involve too many people with competing priorities — city officials who want one thing, developers who want another, residents who want something else — and we find a way forward.

Sometimes that means building a coalition. Sometimes it means presenting data that changes the conversation. Always, it means understanding what each stakeholder actually needs versus what they say they need, and designing something that works for everyone. We come in, get it done, and leave your team with the capacity to sustain the work.

Principles

How we think about the work.

01

Data over anecdote

Decisions grounded in real, on-the-ground evidence are more durable than the loudest voice in the room — so we help communities gather their own.

02

Broaden the circle

We design for the people who usually don't show up. Lower the barrier, make it a little fun, and the whole neighborhood's voice gets into the room.

03

Translate across sectors

We speak fluently between "City Council," "Parking Board," and "Tech Innovation," moving high-stakes projects forward where others stall.

04

Leave capacity behind

Our job is done when your team can sustain the work without us. We build frameworks, train people, and document everything.

05

Presence creates permission

People join when something tells them they're welcome. We build that signal — physically and digitally — before anyone opens an app.

06

Curated, never scraped

A small set of real, verified gatherings and real data beats an algorithmic feed. Trust is the product.

How we work

A typical engagement.

Most engagements run six to twelve months and move through four phases — scoped to your specific problem, not a template.

Discovery & strategy

Stakeholder mapping, discovery interviews, a strategic framework, and the success metrics we'll hold ourselves to.

Design & planning

Program design, a community-engagement plan, a pilot roadmap, and a clear-eyed risk-mitigation strategy.

Implementation

Execution support, a measurement framework, and the tools deployed where they belong — on the ground and on screen.

Knowledge transfer

Team training and documentation so the work keeps going after we step back. We build capacity, not dependence.
