PB Pathways Phase 3

Challenge

A proposed bike boulevard faced intense opposition over parking removal in a highly contested district — a process that had historically blocked similar projects.

Action

Designed and facilitated six community workshops to surface concerns and build consensus, deployed citizen-science traffic and speed tools, and presented data-driven recommendations to multiple approval boards.

Result
  • Only project to pass the Parking Advisory Board with the top approval vote
  • Secured a unanimous Planning Group recommendation
  • Shifted the conversation from parking protection to sustainable mobility

Action

PB Counts

Citizen-science infrastructure

Challenge

Advocacy lacked reliable, repeatable data. Decisions were made on anecdotes, and volunteers had enthusiasm but no framework for systematic collection.

Action

Built repeatable systems spanning traffic studies, speed monitoring, and parking surveys; secured county funding for automated technology; and created train-the-trainer models so volunteers could run surveys independently.

Result
  • 11 years of continuous data — San Diego's longest-running community traffic study
  • County grant secured for automated traffic technology
  • Comprehensive parking data now informs Planning Group housing decisions
