Citizen Science Infrastructure for Pacific Beach

Challenge:

Community advocacy lacked reliable, repeatable data collection methods. Decisions were made on anecdotes rather than evidence. Volunteers had enthusiasm but no framework for systematic data gathering.

Action:

Built comprehensive citizen science infrastructure spanning traffic studies, speed monitoring, and parking surveys. Secured county funding for automated technology. Created train-the-trainer models enabling volunteers to independently conduct surveys. Established replicable frameworks that other neighborhoods could adopt.

Result:
  • 11 years of continuous data collection - longest-running community traffic study in San Diego
  • County grant secured for automated traffic technology, establishing framework for other neighborhoods
  • 6 community workshops facilitated for PB Pathways, engaging broader cross-section than typical meetings
  • Comprehensive parking data now informs Planning Group decisions on housing and development
  • Created train-the-trainer model where volunteers independently conduct surveys with consistent methodology
Key Initiatives:
PB Counts - Traffic Study Program

11 years running. Longest-running community-led traffic study in San Diego. Technologically enhanced with automated traffic counters providing continuous data at key locations. Established framework and county connection for other community groups to obtain similar funding and survey their neighborhoods.
