Enterprise program leadership

Operating at scale without losing the decision.

At PlayStation, I owned and managed implementation projects and planning-cycle operations as Release Train Engineer across a complex commerce organization. My scope was program delivery—not codebase ownership or engineering-team line management.

Sony · Operating model

Quarterly planning became a monthly delivery cadence.

As Release Train Engineer for 6+ Agile teams, I led a remote-first change program across regional, currency, and regulatory complexity. I introduced “remote tables,” led the Rally-to-Jira migration, and improved feature-funnel transparency with Product Management.

Result: a cadence designed around the organization’s actual delivery needs, with remote participation adopted as standard practice.

Sony · High-stakes commerce

An immovable launch met changing operating conditions.

I led the cross-functional implementation of the PlayStation Direct preorder and prerelease checkout flow, coordinating across engineering, product, finance, legal, and external partners while demand, supply, queue behavior, and scalper pressure kept moving.

Delivery context: the wider PS5 launch quarter sold 4.5 million units.

Sony · Innovation systems

Exploration got an evidence path.

Engineering Excellence hackathons brought together 80 to 240 participants across 6+ engineering teams and produced 30+ prototypes per event. I also partnered with IDEO on AR, VR, and tactile low-screen play and used focus-group variant testing to decide what advanced.

Result: one prototype adopted into production reduced cloud infrastructure costs 40%.

Civic and stakeholder leadership

Influence without an org chart.

Public processes expose whether a leader can listen, communicate clearly, hold conflict, and still help a group reach a decision.

Coalition and public approval

PB Pathways Phase 3

Challenge: A contested bike boulevard faced organized opposition and multiple public decision points.

My role: I ran six community workshops, served as primary spokesperson, and coordinated across city, county, nonprofit, Planning Group, and Parking Board stakeholders.

Result: unanimous Planning Group recommendation and Parking Board approval.

Organizational credibility

PB Arts Center

During my presidency, Beautiful PB turned the credibility of its first major county grant into a larger capital campaign, while I scaled the organization’s annual operating budget from under $1,000 to $40,000.

Organizational result: Beautiful PB’s campaign raised $450,000 for capital restoration of the PB Arts Center.

2023–2025 modernization

PB Counts

I did not found the study. I modernized the long-running citizen-science program by securing a $30,000 County of San Diego grant, selecting the vendor, negotiating private sites, and adding three continuous automated counters alongside the 20 to 40 volunteer model.

I also built public visualizations and introduced near-miss conflict data, shifting the conversation from a single annual count toward year-round monitoring and human safety.

Result: continuous data at three locations, public reporting, and a durable automated layer added to an established volunteer program.
