YOUR MENU SHOULD

Most gelato shops have a QR code that links to a static flavor list. Customers scan it, see a wall of text, and panic. What if it linked to this instead?

01
A dynamic menu that guides instead of lists
Your QR sign already tells customers to scan. We replace that static flavor list with an interactive experience — customers answer 4 quick questions and get a personalized cup recommendation built from your actual menu.
02
Help customers order with confidence
The concept is designed to reduce choice overload and make an unfamiliar menu easier to navigate. Any effect on ordering behavior would need to be measured in a real pilot.
03
You keep your menu current, we handle everything else
Update your flavor list through a simple shared spreadsheet. No POS integration. No app to install. No technical help needed. We maintain the tool; you just keep the flavors fresh.
